# Article Visuals

## Visual Assets from Cognitive Drift Institute Articles

### Overview

This directory contains visual assets created for articles, essays, and conceptual publications produced by the Cognitive Drift Institute.

These visuals are designed to communicate ideas related to cognitive drift, recursive thinking, semantic compression, co-cognition, representational instability, and AI-mediated cognition through simplified symbolic and conceptual imagery.

---

## Contents

This directory includes visuals related to topics such as:

- cognitive drift and semantic instability  
- recursive compression and recursive thinking  
- co-cognition and distributed cognition  
- AI-mediated cognition and self-modeling  
- cognitive compression styles  
- semantic overload and meaning degradation  
- symbolic systems and feedback collapse  

---

## Role Within the Cognitive Drift Institute

Article visuals provide the visual communication layer of the Cognitive Drift Institute.

These materials are intended to make abstract cognitive and semantic concepts more legible, compressible, and transferable across essays, presentations, repositories, and AI-native research environments.
