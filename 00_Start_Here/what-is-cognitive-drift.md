#### What Is Cognitive Drift?

*A short introduction to the Cognitive Drift framework.*

---

## Overview

Cognitive Drift describes how human cognition gradually reorganizes under conditions of continual mediation, information overload, optimization pressure, and artificial intelligence.

Human cognition evolved through direct experience, repeated feedback, and relatively stable patterns of meaning. Modern informational environments operate differently. Information increasingly arrives through layers of filtering, algorithmic selection, compression, and reinterpretation before it reaches the mind.

As these layers accumulate, cognition relies more heavily on representations than direct experience. Attention becomes increasingly fragmented, interpretations are revised more frequently, and meaning becomes less stable as information moves through rapidly changing digital environments.

Cognitive Drift describes this structural shift and the informational conditions that produce it.

---

## How Cognitive Drift Emerges

Cognitive Drift commonly develops through three interacting pressures.

### Information Overload

The volume and speed of information exceed the mind's ability to develop stable interpretations.

### Optimization Pressure

Digital platforms, institutions, and AI systems increasingly shape attention and behavior through measurable incentives such as engagement, productivity, and visibility.

### Mediated Experience

A growing proportion of perception occurs through interfaces, algorithms, and representations rather than direct interaction with the world.

Together, these conditions gradually reshape how attention, judgment, and meaning stabilize.

## Areas of Study

The Cognitive Drift framework examines topics including:

- Attention fragmentation
- Information overload
- AI-human co-cognition
- Distributed cognition
- Cognitive hygiene
- Recursive compression
- Judgment under mediation
- Meaning formation in digital environments

---

## Relationship to Reality Drift

Cognitive Drift represents the cognitive layer of the broader Reality Drift framework.

While Reality Drift examines how systems gradually lose alignment with the realities they are intended to represent, Cognitive Drift examines how human cognition adapts within those changing informational environments.
