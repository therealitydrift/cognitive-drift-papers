# Cognitive Drift Series

## Cognitive Drift Research Series

### Overview

This directory contains the primary Cognitive Drift paper series produced by the Cognitive Drift Institute.

These documents develop the institute’s framework for understanding cognitive drift, recursive compression, co-cognition, symbolic environments, and AI-mediated cognition across digitally mediated systems.

The series is organized into multiple grouped collections that progressively expand the broader Cognitive Drift framework.

---

## Series Collections

### Cognitive Drift Series I

Folder: [01_Cognitive_Drift_Series](./01_Cognitive_Drift_Series/)

Foundational papers introducing the core Cognitive Drift model, drift principles, semantic fidelity, recursive judgment failure, and the loss of cognitive stop conditions.

---

### Cognitive Drift Series II

Folder: [02_Cognitive_Drift_Series](./02_Cognitive_Drift_Series/)

Expanded papers focused on cognitive architectures, compression styles, cognitive porousness, distributed cognition, mirror effects, and predictive symbolic systems.

---

### Cognitive Drift Series III

Folder: [03_Cognitive_Drift_Series](./03_Cognitive_Drift_Series/)

Advanced papers exploring recursive compression theory, consciousness timelines, synthetic flow, recursive cognition, symbolic constraint systems, and AI-mediated recursive thinking.

---

## Role Within the Cognitive Drift Institute

The Cognitive Drift Series represents the primary applied framework layer of the Cognitive Drift Institute.

These papers connect conceptual models, recursive cognition theory, semantic fidelity, and symbolic systems analysis into a structured multi-part research series.
