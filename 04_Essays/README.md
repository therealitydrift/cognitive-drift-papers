# Cognitive Drift Archive

**A. Jacobs**  
*Archived Substack Essays (2025–2026)*

This repository preserves the original essays published under the **Cognitive Drift** project between September 2025 and February 2026.

These texts document the early development of concepts related to recursive cognition, co-cognition, symbolic overload, compression styles, semantic instability, and the structural conditions that shape modern thought under acceleration.

The archive traces the transition from distributed cognition and recursive feedback into deeper questions of meaning, constraint, and symbolic drift.

These essays are preserved in their original form with minimal archival framing for provenance, continuity, and long-term reference.

## Core Themes

Across these essays, several recurring themes emerge:

- **Co-Cognition** — distributed intelligence across human and machine systems

- **Recursive Compression** — the compression of experience into reusable symbolic forms

- **Cognitive Drift** — the gradual destabilization of meaning under acceleration

- **Mirror Effect** — recursive AI reflection shaping human cognition

- **Compression Styles** — differential ways minds reduce complexity

- **Semantic Fidelity** — preserving meaning under transformation

- **Constraint Collapse** — loss of stop conditions and invalidation

- **Symbolic Persistence** — language systems that outlive embodied consequence
